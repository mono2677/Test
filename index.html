About us

Who we are

The Cambridge Ophthalmology Student Society represents students interested in eye health, visual neuroscience, and ophthalmic surgery. We work closely with local ophthalmologists and trainees.

We aim to make ophthalmology accessible, visible, and achievable for Cambridge students at every stage of medical school. Whether you are simply curious about the specialty or already planning a career in ophthalmology, we want to help you:

  • discover what ophthalmology practice actually looks like;
  • find teaching, research, and elective opportunities in Cambridge and beyond;
  • prepare for exams such as the Duke Elder and clinical OSCE stations;
  • connect with trainees and consultants for informal advice and mentorship.

What we do
  • Teaching: OSCE skills evenings, case-based discussions, and anatomy refreshers.
  • Events: advertising local grand rounds, regional meetings, and national conferences.
  • Resources Hub: concise blog posts, condition-of-the-week, and curated learning links.
  • Duke Elder support: weekly quiz and signposting to high-yield material.
